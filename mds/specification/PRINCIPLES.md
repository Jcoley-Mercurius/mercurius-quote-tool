# Mercurius Quote Tool — Design Principles

Version: MDS v1.0  
Status: Active

## Make the money path unmistakable

Show Marketplace or Direct source, customer total, payment recipient/path, commission relevance, expiration, and the next legitimate action before commitment. Pipeline value is not realized revenue.

## Move work forward

Prioritize attention, deadlines, valid next actions, recovery, and saved-state clarity over decorative dashboard metrics. Every empty, error, and placeholder state provides one safe next step.

## One system with context-aware behavior

Marketplace and Direct work share primitives, shells, and workflows. Source labels and policy-aware content change; the visual brand and component system do not split into separate products.

## Professional at the job site

Design for one-handed mobile use, interrupted work, weak connectivity, photos, long customer data, and consequential decisions. Preserve 44px targets, safe areas, autosave, and recovery.

## AI supports judgment

AI-generated scope or pricing is review-required. Coral identifies explicit AI generation; it never disguises automation as a verified result. Manual editing and safe retry remain available.

## Accessibility is structural

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA in design and implementation. Semantic order, focus, error recovery, text alternatives, responsive reflow, and non-gesture controls are required behavior—not a finishing pass.
